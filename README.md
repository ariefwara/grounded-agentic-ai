# Grounded Agentic AI

Grounded Agentic AI is a control layer for AI-generated business communication.

It is designed for organizations that want AI to help respond to customers, employees, partners, or other audiences without allowing the model to answer freely outside approved business boundaries.

## Problem

AI can make communication faster, but business communication often carries risk. A response may sound helpful while still being unsupported, inconsistent with policy, written in the wrong tone, or unsuitable for the person asking.

Grounded Agentic AI focuses on a simple business question:

> Should this AI response be allowed?

## Approach

The system is built around approved business context, controlled response behavior, and reviewable decisions. It helps ensure that AI responses stay aligned with the information, audience, and communication rules defined by the organization.
